Interactive systems

Installations that respond

For museums, exhibitions and events I make ordinary objects interactive: a desk, a set of mechanisms, a board. Visitors don't just look, they touch, open, move, and the object responds. I design the whole chain, from sensors to actuators, through electronics, firmware and software. Visitors take home a memory that is hard to forget and easy to share.

About

Roberto Luttino

I started programming on the Commodore 64 and soldering components in my teenage “electronics lab”. Then MS-DOS, Amiga, Windows, and finally Linux and embedded systems.

At the MultiDams in Turin, between music and theatre, I came across interactive systems: the place where electronics, music and computing meet. That is where MindContact began. Today I mostly build custom software, with the same experimental approach.
